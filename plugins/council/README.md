# Council

The Council is a seven-agent panel with a three-person advisory panel picked for each idea. It reviews any idea (a startup, a feature, a company decision or a life choice) and returns a rated verdict.

## Agents (each can be called on its own)

- **researcher**: builds a neutral factual baseline. For technical ideas it searches code and the web. For business ideas it does market research. For other ideas it gathers the relevant facts.
- **believer**: makes the strongest evidence-based case for the idea.
- **skeptic**: attacks the idea and ranks weaknesses as Fatal, Serious or Manageable.
- **investor**: looks only at profitability and ROI, then decides Invest, Invest with conditions or Pass.
- **recruiter**: picks three real industry figures (an operator, an investor or strategist, and a domain expert or contrarian) matched to the idea, and writes a researched Persona Brief for each.
- **advisor**: gives one panelist's simulated perspective, grounded in their public record and clearly labeled as a simulation. Runs three times in parallel during the council.
- **judge**: gives a weighted rating out of 10, a verdict and next steps.

Any agent works without the others. When it's missing inputs, such as a Research Brief, it does its own quick research.

## Skill

- **council**: runs the full panel in order. The Researcher and the Recruiter go first, then the Believer, the Skeptic, the Investor (when the idea is a business matter) and the three Advisors run in parallel, and the Judge goes last.

Try: "Run the council on my idea for …", or call an agent directly: "Have the skeptic tear apart …".
